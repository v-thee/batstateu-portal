About TNEU

The National Engineering University

Batangas State University (BatStateU) is the National Engineering University of the Philippines, committed to producing globally competitive graduates and advancing research and innovation. With a rich history spanning over 100 years, we continue to lead in engineering, technology, and the sciences.

Our TNEU Portal connects students, faculty, and partners to a seamless digital experience — from admissions to academic resources, all in one place.
